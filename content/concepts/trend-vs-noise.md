## **Understanding Trend vs Noise**

Progress doesn’t unfold in perfect straight lines — it unfolds step by step.  
Think of your body data like walking toward a destination:

> **Noise is each individual step. Trend is the direction of your journey.**

Some steps feel strong, others feel shaky.  
Some days you move forward, some sideways, and some barely at all.  
But no single step defines your progress. What matters is the **overall direction** you’re moving in.

Formetrix helps you focus on the journey — the long‑term movement — rather than the moment‑to‑moment fluctuations that naturally appear along the way.

---

## **How Noise Happens**

Individual measurements are inherently noisy. Short‑term changes often reflect:

- hydration shifts
- food intake
- glycogen levels
- sleep and stress
- measurement technique variability

These fluctuations are **not** fat gain or fat loss — they’re normal biological variation.

---

## **What Noise Looks Like**

- Day‑to‑day ups and downs
- Isolated spikes or drops
- Changes that reverse quickly

Noise is expected — and unavoidable.

---

## **What a Trend Looks Like**

- Directional change across **multiple measurements**
- Consistent movement in related metrics (e.g., weight, waist, body‑fat estimate)
- Stability across weeks, not days

Trends emerge **over time**, not from single data points.

---

## **How to Interpret Correctly**

- Don’t react to a single reading
- Compare values under similar conditions
- Allow trends to form before drawing conclusions

> [!success] The Bigger Picture
>Progress is rarely obvious in the moment. It becomes visible only when you step back and look at the path you’ve been walking.
