# **Formetrix AI — Local, Private, and Context-Aware Intelligence**

## **What Formetrix AI Is**

Formetrix AI is not a single model, feature, or chatbot.

It is an **umbrella system** that coordinates multiple AI capabilities across the app — including reasoning, estimation, analysis, and future vision-based tools — while respecting scope, privacy, and context.

At its core, Formetrix AI combines:

- local language models
    
- structured routing and orchestration
    
- domain-specific tools (measurements, predictors, analysis)
    
- controlled knowledge access (curated and personal)

>[!Important]
The goal is not to “talk back,” but to **reason within the Formetrix system**.
  
Formetrix AI is a **local-first intelligence layer** designed to help you interpret measurements, understand trends, and plan next steps — while keeping your data **private, controlled, and contextual**.

>[!important]
Rather than sending your health data to external services, Formetrix AI is built to work **where your data already lives**: on your device.

---
## **Why Local AI Comes First**

> [!Important] TL;DR
> Your data belongs to you.
> Local **AI ensures** it stays that way.

Health and body data are deeply personal. Formetrix deliberately chooses a **local-first AI approach** so that:

- your measurements are not sent to corporations or third parties
    
- your data is not indexed, logged, or profiled externally
    
- your body data remains **yours**, by default

This design gives you the freedom to:

- track honestly and completely
    
- input sensitive information without hesitation
    
- experiment, analyze, and reflect without surveillance

Cloud-based systems may be powerful, but they introduce unavoidable tradeoffs — especially when health data is involved. Formetrix avoids those tradeoffs wherever possible.

---
## **Inference, Knowledge, and Freshness**

Formetrix AI separates **reasoning** from **knowledge**.

- **Inference** (reasoning, interpretation, explanation) happens locally
    
- **Knowledge updates** (guidelines, curated references, models) can be retrieved selectively

This means:

- your private data is never used to “fetch answers” from the internet
    
- the system can stay up to date without exposing your measurements
    
- intelligence improves without sacrificing privacy

When external knowledge is used, it is **curated and controlled**, not scraped or inferred from your personal data.

---

## **AI Capability Scales With Your Device**

Formetrix AI adapts to the hardware you already own.

More capable devices can run larger models locally, producing:

- stronger reasoning
    
- better nuance and explanation
    
- higher accuracy in complex contexts

Less powerful devices still receive:

- stable, usable AI assistance
    
- consistent behavior and interpretation
    
- the same privacy guarantees

>[!warning] Reliability is Number One Priority
> Higher-capacity models generally produce **better results**,
> but the system always prioritizes **reliability and usability** over raw performance.
>>You are not required to understand models or configurations — Formetrix will handle it automatically for you.

## **Indicative Model Capability Levels**

Rather than locking users into a specific model, Formetrix supports **tiers of local inference quality**, selected automatically based on device capability.

| **AI Engine Tier**        | **Typical Device Class**         | **What Improves**                         |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Formetrix AI Base**     | Older phones / low-power devices | Core reasoning, stable interpretation     |
| **Formetrix AI Standard** | Modern phones & tablets          | Clear explanations, trend analysis        |
| **Formetrix AI Advanced** | Mid-range desktops               | Deeper context, better planning           |
| **Formetrix AI Ultra**    | High-end desktops                | Highest reasoning depth, complex analysis |

All levels operate **locally** and respect the same privacy boundaries.

## **Continual Learning & Adaptation**

>Formetrix AI is designed to **adapt with you the longer you use it**.

Rather than changing the underlying model, Formetrix applies **Continual Learning Strategies** and **Continual Context Refinement** to become more relevant over time — without compromising stability or privacy.

This happens through:

- deeper understanding of your profile and measurement history
    
- structured interpretation of new measurements as they are added
    
- increasingly precise use of **your own context** during reasoning 

Your data is used to **inform how the Formetrix AI System reasons**, interprets, and prioritizes information _within your personal context_.

With this approach, Formetrix aims to:

- personalize intelligence **without introducing concept drift**
    
- keep historical measurements interpretable and comparable
    
- reflect changes in goals, habits, or body state naturally over time

> [!Important] **You are the customer — not the product**
> Formetrix AI learns **about you**, not _from you_ in a way that affects others.

> [!success] **The longer you use it, the better it fits**
> Over time, Formetrix AI becomes more **specialized, contextual, and accurate**, while remaining stable, private, and predictable.
